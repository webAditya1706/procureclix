FAQs

FAQs about Procurement Management Software FAQs

ProcureClix is a comprehensive e-procurement software platform that helps organizations automate and streamline their entire purchasing process. Our digital procurement solution combines strategic sourcing, spend analytics, and supplier management meant to streamline your overall operations with powerful automation capabilities.

ProcureClix drives procurement transformation for organizations in over 12 countries globally, with implementations that cater to manufacturing, healthcare, financial services, and the public sector.

ProcureClix assists organizations to tackle serious procurement risks such as unauthorized expenditure, non-compliance by suppliers, contract leakage, payment fraud, and non-compliance with regulations. Our entire procurement lifecycle visibility facilitates proactive risk management and monitoring of compliance.

Our easy-to-use workflow engine for requisitions enables you to set up multi-level approval hierarchies with conditional logic, delegation, and mobile approvals. This simplifies the approval process while still maintaining proper controls and documentation for audit.

Yes, ProcureClix automates the complete purchase order lifecycle. The system can generate POs automatically from approved requisitions, route them to suppliers, track acknowledgments, manage changes, and link POs to receiving and invoice records for three-way matching.

Our contract management functions offer a central repository for every procurement agreement with automated tracking of milestones, renewal reminders, and compliance checks. The system integrates with e-signature tools such as DocuSign and SignNow to process faster execution and has a real-time dashboard of the total contracted amount by categories, locations, and departments.

Our AI-powered spend analytics engine aggregates data from multiple sources, performs thorough cleansing, and creates detailed categorization using UNSPSC or custom taxonomy. This provides 100% visibility into spend patterns, supplier performance, and savings opportunities across your organization.
