FAQs about RFQ Software

RFP, RFI, and RFQ software is a procurement application that helps businesses automate the quote collection process and manage vendor responses efficiently. Instead of juggling emails and spreadsheets, this application helps businesses collect and evaluate supplier responses, compare bids, and make smarter purchasing decisions—faster and with fewer errors.

An RFP (Request for Proposal) is used when you need vendors to propose solutions, including their approach and pricing. It’s ideal for services or complex projects. An RFQ (Request for Quotation) is used when you need pricing for specific products or services. It focuses purely on cost comparison.

In short, an RFP response is usually used when solutions and methodologies are being sought, while an RFQ is used when only quotes for specific products or services are needed.
