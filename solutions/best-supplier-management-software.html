Supplier lifecycle management (SLM) refers to the process of managing a supplier's relationship with an organization from beginning to end. It involves selecting, onboarding, managing, evaluating, and offboarding a supplier.

ProcureClix is designed to strengthen supplier relationships through several key features:
  1. Supplier performance scorecards that track quality, delivery, and responsiveness
  2. Collaborative communication portals for direct engagement with vendors
  3. Structured autonomous onboarding processes that establish clear expectations
  4. Feedback mechanisms allowing two-way communication
By fostering transparency and accountability, ProcureClix transforms vendor interactions from transactional exchanges into strategic partnerships that drive mutual growth.
