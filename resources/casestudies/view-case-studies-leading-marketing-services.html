Case Study

Company Profile

Leading marketing services company with global presence delivers creative, production and technology services to major multinational brands. The company leverages e-procurement technology and services to save time, reduce costs and streamline the procurement process for clients.

ProcureClix Solutions and Services Utilized
  • Strategic Sourcing – e-Sourcing, eAuction, Optimization
  • Integration Services – Customization

Summary

The Marketing Services company needed to integrate e-sourcing capabilities into its existing procurement process and then drive consistent usage across disparate global business units.

Through ProcureClix, the company quickly added a new capability that allowed it to deliver better value to its clients. With ProcureClix’s robust e-sourcing solution offering and its customization services, the company leveraged auction events to save up to 18% in cost of print services sourced from its network of suppliers.

Business objective

The company needed to streamline the procurement process and enable competitive sourcing for productive services.

Procurement challenges

The company utilized an existing platform to procure services from its network of suppliers. However, this system was designed to only manage the purchase order and requisitioning process and did not have the strategic sourcing capabilities needed to drive savings and improve efficiencies.

The company wanted to add e-sourcing capabilities to their procurement process.

The ability to integrate e-sourcing into their existing platform was an important requirement, to ensure minimal disruption and continued utilization of their existing platform and capabilities.

Selecting ProcureClix to meet their procurement challenges

The client explored a range of e-procurement solution providers and selected ProcureClix as the best fit for its need, for several reasons:

Key Steps in a Strategic Sourcing Process
  1. ProcureClix demonstrated the ability to integrate their e-procurement solution into the client’s existing platform, by leveraging its core APIs and with basic customization to create a seamless user experience.
  2. ProcureClix’s technology platform provided the advanced functionality that was essential to implementing the procurement requirements. For example, the capability to apply transformational bidding, using configurable custom fields and a powerful rules engine to execute events specific to the marketing service industry.
  3. The ease-of-use of the ProcureClix platform would enable client to drive consistent and high usage of its new e-sourcing capabilities.

ProcureClix Advantage

Ease of integration into existing system and processes to reduce time-to-savings:

  • Provided the ability to seamlessly integrate into homegrown system, to create a unified user experience, running auctions from their internal system
  • Leveraging the in-built web-services integration capability of ProcureClix

Advanced capability to conduct events specific to the marketing services

  • Transformational bidding
  • Configurable custom fields and a powerful rules engine

Easy-to-use solution to increase usage of e-sourcing

  • The client conducted 15 auctions to procure print services during its pilot, which achieved cost savings of up to 18%

Results from Strategic Sourcing Adoption

The ProcureClix solution offered the ease-of- integration to leverage client’s existing business process and the advanced capability to meet unique needs of the marketing services industry

ProcureClix e-Sourcing Suite utilized to source services across multiple global business units

The Marketing Services company realized savings ranging from 12% to 18% in procurement costs for printing services
