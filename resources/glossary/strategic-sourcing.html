Strategic Sourcing

Strategic sourcing in procurement is a holistic, long-term approach to sourcing goods and services that focuses on maximizing value, not just minimizing cost. Unlike traditional sourcing, which is often reactive and price-driven, strategic sourcing involves deep analysis, supplier collaboration, and alignment with organizational goals.


Key Principles of Strategic Sourcing:
  1. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) – Focuses on all costs (purchase, maintenance, delivery, disposal), not just price.
  2. Data-Driven Decisions – Uses spend analysis, market intelligence, and performance metrics.
  3. Supplier Relationship Management – Builds strong partnerships for innovation, risk reduction, and continuous improvement.
  4. Cross-Functional Teams – Involves stakeholders from finance, operations, legal, etc., to align sourcing with business needs.
  5. Continuous Improvement – Regularly evaluates sourcing strategies to adapt to changing market conditions.
Strategic Sourcing Process (Typical 7 Steps):
  1. Analyze Spend – Understand where and how money is being spent.
  2. Assess Supply Market – Research supplier capabilities, trends, and risks.
  3. Develop Sourcing Strategy – Define sourcing objectives and approach (e.g., single vs. multiple suppliers).
  4. Issue RFX (RFP, RFQ, etc.) – Solicit proposals or quotes from suppliers.
  5. Evaluate & Select Suppliers – Compare based on price, quality, capacity, and strategic fit.
  6. Negotiate & Contract – Finalize terms and create a contract.
  7. Monitor & Optimize – Track supplier performance and adapt the strategy over time.
Benefits of Strategic Sourcing:
  • Long-term cost savings
  • Risk mitigation
  • Supplier innovation and value creation
  • Better alignment with business objectives
