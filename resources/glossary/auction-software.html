What is Auction Software?

Auction software in procurement is a digital tool used to run reverse auctions or e-auctions, where suppliers compete in real time to offer the lowest price for goods or services that a buyer wants to purchase.

Key Features of Auction Software:
  • Real-time bidding: Suppliers submit live bids, often competing to undercut each other’s prices.
  • Automated rules: Set auction types (e.g., English, Dutch, Japanese), time limits, and bid increments.
  • Transparency and analytics: Tracks bidding history and provides post-auction reports for analysis.
  • Supplier management: Allows pre-qualification and communication with vendors.
Types of Auctions Commonly Used:
  1. 1. Reverse Auction– Suppliers lower their prices to win a buyer’s business (most common in procurement).
  2. 2. Dutch Auction– Price drops over time until a supplier accepts the current price.
  3. 3. English Auction– Suppliers increase their bids, used more in asset selling than buying.
Benefits:
  • Drives competitive pricing
  • Improves transparency and fairness
  • Reduces negotiation time
  • Enhances procurement efficiency
